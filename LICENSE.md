# Licensing Policy

> **Status: Decision Pending Stakeholder Review**

The final software licensing terms for the **Classroom Platform** project have not yet been determined.

---

## 1. Pending License Determination

Prior to public release or commercial distribution, the project governance team will select an appropriate licensing framework. Potential candidates under evaluation include:

* **Permissive Open Source**: Apache 2.0 or MIT License for broad community adoption and extensibility.
* **Reciprocal Open Source**: AGPLv3 or European Union Public Licence (EUPL) to guarantee that cloud-hosted service providers return improvements to the community.
* **Dual Licensing**: Community open-source edition coupled with a commercial/enterprise license for proprietary extensions and managed support.
